Increment mode

Nudge the number under the caret without leaving the keyboard. Each shortcut is a single character.

Fixed gestures — not configurable

←→
Move to the previous / next number on the current line.
↑↓
Move to the line above / below, on its first number.
a
Add the active number to the selection, or take it out.
d
Clear the whole selection.
Esc
Leave increment mode.
Click

Shift + click
